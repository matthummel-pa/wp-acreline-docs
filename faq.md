# FAQ

**Where do I buy it?**  
[matthummel.com/product/acreline](https://matthummel.com/product/acreline/) or [matthummel.com/shop](https://matthummel.com/shop/).

**What does a license include?**  
A year of updates and email support for 1 site ($59.99), 5 sites ($99.99), or unlimited sites ($199.99). Features keep working if the license expires. You stop receiving updates and support until you renew.

**Is the demo a real brokerage?**  
No. [acreline.matthummel.com](https://acreline.matthummel.com/) is fiction. Sample phones use `555`. Sample emails use `@acreline-concept.test`.

**Does it connect to the MLS?**  
No. You enter listings yourself. An MLS number is a field, not a feed.

**Do showings go on my calendar?**  
They become Booking posts in WordPress. You confirm them. An agent profile can link out to your own calendar.

**Can I edit the header in the Site Editor?**  
No. Full-site editing is off. Use the Customizer for identity and Acreline Settings for listing, agent, and showing options. Pages and posts use the block editor.

**Why is there a “Get this theme” bar on the demo?**  
That bar is only on the concept site. It is not in the theme you install.

**Where is the theme source?**  
The theme source is `matthummel-pa/wp-acreline`. These guides are the public documentation.
