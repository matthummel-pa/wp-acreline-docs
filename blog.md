# The blog

**Posts** and **pages** use the block editor. Write the guide, neighborhood notes, and announcements there.

**Listings, agents, and bookings** stay on classic field boxes. Do not look for those fields inside the block editor.

The theme does not turn on full-site editing. You edit content in the post or page, and site chrome (phone, address, colors) in the Customizer and Acreline Settings. You do not design the header in the Site Editor.

The concept blog on the demo site is sample copy. Replace it.
