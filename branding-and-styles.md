# Branding and styles

Acreline ships with named color styles (paper, ink, and accent). Pick one in **Appearance → Customize** or on the General tab of Acreline Settings. The public site can show a style switcher so a visitor can preview styles. That choice is saved in the browser (`ks-color-scheme`). It does not change the saved site default for other visitors.

Use your own logo through **Appearance → Customize → Site Identity**, the standard WordPress logo control.

The concept demo uses a light paper background, dark ink text, and a single accent. Do not switch the marketing pages to a dark theme. Cards stay on a white face.

## Purchase bar on the concept demo

[acreline.matthummel.com](https://acreline.matthummel.com/) may show a bar with style chips and a **Get this theme** button. That bar is the Acreline demo-bar plugin, installed only on the concept site. **Get this theme** opens [matthummel.com/product/acreline](https://matthummel.com/product/acreline/). The style chips use the same styles as the theme switcher. Closing the bar lasts for the browser session.

Buyer sites do not include that bar. The theme zip does not contain it.

The theme’s own fiction line (concept demo, sample data) stays on the concept site. It is separate from the purchase bar.
